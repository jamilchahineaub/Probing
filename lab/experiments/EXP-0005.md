# EXP-0005 — Fixed-chirp decision sufficiency

## Scientific question

> Can one fixed low-mid-frequency chirp reliably estimate task-relevant
> interaction properties and classify whether sustained contact is safe,
> without requiring accurate separate estimates of `k`, `c`, and `m_eff`?

## Frozen protocol

- One nonadaptive unilateral 0.5 N chirp from 0.5 to 5 Hz for 3 s.
- Synchronized 200 Hz causal-low-pass sensing at low, nominal, and high noise.
- Continuous log-space population: `k = 75–2500 N/m`, `c = 0.3–60 Ns/m`, and
  `m_eff = 0.10–6 kg`.
- Disjoint targets: 240 training cases on seeds 5101–5120, 120 calibration
  cases on 5201–5210, and 240 untouched validation cases on 6101–6120.
- Hidden future maneuver: raised-cosine loading to 2 N, sustained hold,
  unloading, and ringdown. Validation predictions were frozen before this
  maneuver was simulated.
- SAFE/CAUTION/UNSAFE labels came only from future-response displacement,
  velocity, late-hold oscillation, settling, and a 20 mm contact-travel proxy.
- Primary predictor: ridge response models using estimated stiffness plus
  causal frequency- and time-domain probe features. Estimated damping and mass
  were excluded.
- SAFE/UNSAFE decisions used calibrated one-sided response bounds. The primary
  limit was false-safe rate at or below 5% in every noise regime.

## Reference run

- Run ID: `EXP-0005_20260829T191551.247939Z_s6101_8de417c0`.
- 7,920 held-out predictor rows: 240 targets, three noise regimes, and eleven
  predictor/feature-set combinations.
- All ten integrity checks passed. Validation predictions were generated before
  the hidden future response was joined.
- EXP-0001 through EXP-0004 artifact fingerprints were unchanged before and
  after execution.
- Validation probes had zero configured safety events. One training target
  exceeded the `0.5 m/s` velocity limit by `0.000194 m/s`; this is retained in
  the safety log and is not hidden.
- 75 tests passed before the held-out run.

## Primary safety classification

| Noise | Accuracy | False-safe rate | False-safe count | Unsafe recall |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Low | 85.83% | 2.60% | 2/77 non-safe | 84.31% |
| Nominal | 86.25% | 3.90% | 3/77 non-safe | 84.31% |
| High | 87.08% | **6.49%** | 5/77 non-safe | 84.31% |

Accuracy passed its nominal and high-noise thresholds. The high-noise
false-safe rate exceeded 5%, and unsafe recall remained below the declared 85%
minimum by one validation case in every regime.

All three nominal false-safe cases were slow-settling UNSAFE targets. Their
actual displacement, velocity, and late-hold oscillation remained within the
SAFE envelope, but settling took `2.932–3.000 s`. The calibrated predicted
settling upper bounds were only `1.538–1.956 s`. This identifies a specific
architectural blind spot: probe sensing ends at 3 s and contains no deliberate
post-chirp zero-force observation period.

## Quantitative future-response prediction

Nominal combined-feature point prediction:

| Quantity | Median absolute error | p95 absolute error | Relative median / p95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Peak displacement | 0.209 mm | 2.605 mm | 4.84% / 17.23% |
| Peak velocity | 0.00055 m/s | 0.00856 m/s | 3.89% / 14.85% |
| Dominant ringdown frequency | 0.393 Hz | 2.707 Hz | 12.71% / 219.28% |
| Late-hold oscillation RMS | 0.00026 mm | 0.782 mm | 11.25% / 263.99% |
| Settling time | 0.063 s | 1.100 s | relative error not meaningful for 139 zero-settling cases |
| Force/displacement gain | 0.104 mm/N | 1.303 mm/N | 4.84% / 17.23% |
| Disturbance-limited force | 0.326 N | 0.940 N | 14.70% / 48.00% |

Peak displacement and gain were the most predictable task quantities.
Resonance, oscillation tails, settling, and safe force had important tail
failures.

The nominal peak-displacement upper bound covered `96.25%` of cases, but low
noise covered only `94.58%`, below the 95% criterion. The safe-force lower bound
covered `94.17%`, `93.75%`, and `95.00%` at low, nominal, and high noise. It is
therefore not yet a reliable 95%-coverage maximum-force certificate. The
contact-travel proxy was detected in 33/34 nominal cases (`97.06%` recall), with
six false alarms.

## Which probe information mattered

Nominal outcome-bound accuracy by feature set:

| Feature set | Accuracy | False-safe rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Estimated `[k,c,m_eff]` | 78.33% | 0.00% |
| Stiffness only | 45.42% | 2.60% |
| Frequency response | 79.17% | 6.49% |
| Time domain | 76.25% | 1.30% |
| Combined task features | **86.25%** | 3.90% |

Stiffness alone was insufficient. Frequency and time response were
complementary, and the combined set gave the highest overall accuracy. The
full-parameter baseline was more conservative on the primary false-safe metric,
so EXP-0005 does not justify deleting physical parameters from the model or
claiming unconditional dominance of the task-feature predictor.

Accurate mass was not required for many correct decisions. In the worst
effective-mass-error quartile, median mass error was `32.94%`, decision accuracy
was `80.00%`, and no nominal false-safe occurred. For the 34 cases above 30%
mass error, accuracy was `85.29%` with no false-safe. Across the full nominal
population, mass error median/p95 was `9.55%/52.71%`; damping was much worse at
`23.39%/382.45%`, while stiffness remained `3.44%/7.35%`.

## Decision

The predeclared decision-sufficiency kill criterion fired:

- accuracy: pass;
- false-safe rate: fail at high noise;
- unsafe recall: fail narrowly in all regimes;
- displacement upper-bound coverage: fail at low noise;
- safe-force lower-bound coverage: fail at low and nominal noise.

One fixed chirp is useful but not reliable enough to certify sustained-contact
safety under the full sensing envelope. Stage 1 remains
`CONTINUE_STAGE_1`; independent MATLAB/Simulink validation is not authorized.
Do not retune the thresholds or predictor on seeds 6101–6120.

The single most useful next experiment is the same bounded chirp followed by a
predeclared zero-force causal observation window. It adds duration but no probe
energy and directly tests whether post-probe ringdown resolves the observed
slow-settling false-safe cases.
