# EXP-0007 — Locked-policy independent replication

EXP-0007 froze the EXP-0006 0.5–5 Hz chirp plus 0.5 s causal passive ring-down policy and evaluated it once on an independent continuous target population. No fitting, calibration, threshold, feature, or policy changes were permitted.

## Result

- 1,360 targets: 960 broad and 400 safety-boundary enriched; 100% new seeds.
- Nominal false-safe rate: **0.192%** (1/520 non-safe cases), one-sided 95% upper bound 0.907%.
- High-noise false-safe rate: **0.384%** (2/521), one-sided 95% upper bound 1.203%.
- Binary SAFE/NON-SAFE accuracy: 88.75% nominal, 89.04% high noise.
- Boundary false-safe rate: 0% nominal, 0.5% high noise.
- No probe safety events; predictions were generated before hidden maneuver outcomes were joined.

The predeclared Stage 1 replication gate passes: `PASS_TO_INDEPENDENT_MATLAB_SIMULINK`. Exact recovery of `[k,c,m_eff]` remains unnecessary for this locked binary safety decision.

Artifacts and plots are in the run manifest and indexed results tables/figures.
